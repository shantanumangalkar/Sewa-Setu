How to apply

1 Mobile OTP
Verify your mobile number with OTP. Instant verification ensures your application security.
2 Citizen Details
Fill the application form (personal details, address, bank details) accurately.
3 Document Upload
Upload your age proof document (JPG or PDF, maximum 5 MB) clearly showing your date of birth.
4 Acknowledgment
Submit the declaration. Note down your Application Number and download PDF acknowledgment.
